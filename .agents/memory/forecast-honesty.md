---
name: Forecast honesty
description: Financial Insights forecast copy and coach explanations must reflect the underlying data source.
---

Treat Financial Insights forecast amounts and monthly comparisons as illustrative sample data until a real forecasting pipeline exists. A coach may explain how to read them, but should not claim to have predicted the user's finances or analyzed bank history.

**Why:** Fixed sample months and category amounts can look like personalized financial advice when paired with a lively coach avatar. Clear labeling prevents users from making decisions based on invented certainty.

**How to apply:** Whenever adding predictions, savings opportunities, or coach copy, verify the data source first. Distinguish sample estimates from actual history or model outputs; only claim live personalization when the underlying calculation supports it.
