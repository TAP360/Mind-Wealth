# MindWealth AI Mobile Application Design Prompt

## Project Overview

Design a world-class mobile application called **MindWealth AI**.

MindWealth AI is an AI-powered Behavioral Finance and Financial Wellness platform designed for Egypt and GCC markets.

Unlike traditional banking or investment apps, MindWealth helps users improve their relationship with money through:

* Financial Personality Profiling
* Emotional Financial Intelligence (EFI)
* AI Financial Coaching
* Smart Behavioral Nudges
* Adaptive Savings Optimization
* Financial Planning
* Investment Guidance
* Loan Management
* Financial Wellness Tracking

The platform combines Behavioral Psychology, Artificial Intelligence, Financial Planning, and Emotional Intelligence to help users build healthier financial habits and make better financial decisions.

The experience should feel like:

* Personal Financial Therapist
* AI Financial Coach
* Trusted Advisor
* Wellness Companion

Not a trading app.
